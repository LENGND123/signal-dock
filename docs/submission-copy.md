## One-line tagline

SignalDock turns buried delivery commitments in team chat into evidence-backed, human-approved decisions—using Switch as the shared collaboration layer.

## Short project description

SignalDock is a Switch-powered delivery-risk control room for project teams. A person posts a client request or delivery concern in a normal team-chat room. Scout extracts commitments and missing facts; Verifier separates evidence from assumptions and scores risk; Coordinator turns that work into 2–3 decision options and waits for a human approval. The final handoff includes an owner, first next step, and an audit line showing the decision was made by a person in the room.

Switch is not a wrapper here: it is where humans and agents share context, hand off work by role, and preserve the approval trail. The same provider-neutral template runs in Switch’s bundled Mattermost environment or a connected Slack, Teams, Discord, or Telegram channel.
