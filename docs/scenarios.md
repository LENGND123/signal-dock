# Demo scenarios

Use these messages in a real SignalDock room. They are fictional and safe to show on video.

## Scenario A — Aurora billing export (primary)

```text
Client Aurora needs the billing export by Friday 5 PM. The reporting API is still returning partial data and Priya is out until Thursday. What should we do?
```

Expected proof points:

- Scout captures the deadline, dependency, availability issue, and one unknown.
- Verifier marks the schedule risk high, differentiates facts from the assumption that Priya is the only owner, and states what could disprove that risk.
- Coordinator recommends a validated partial export, then asks for a human decision.
- You reply: `approve option B`.

## Scenario B — Launch-blocking consent copy (backup)

```text
The retail onboarding launch is planned for Wednesday. Legal says the consent copy may need changes, but the final wording is not back yet. Sam believes we can ship without it. What do we commit to in today’s customer call?
```

Expected proof points:

- Visible separation between a fact (legal review incomplete) and an assumption (shipping without it is allowed).
- Options that protect the customer without letting an agent invent legal advice.
- Human approval before any commitment is framed as final.

## Recording tips

1. Start on the real Switch room, not the visual dashboard.
2. Let each agent’s role label remain visible as it posts.
3. Pause briefly on the Decision Pack and read the approval prompt aloud.
4. Type the approval yourself. This is the proof of human control.
5. End on the dashboard’s completed audit trail for a clean visual recap.
